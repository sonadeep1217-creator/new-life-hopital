Our Services

Comprehensive diabetes care under one roof — consultation, diagnostics, foot care, nutrition & emergency

Diabetes Consultation

Detailed evaluation by senior diabetologists for Type 1, Type 2, LADA, MODY and Gestational Diabetes. Personalized medication, insulin titration and lifestyle advice.

Laboratory & Diagnostics

HbA1c, Fasting & PP Blood Sugar, Lipid Profile, Kidney Function (eGFR, Creatinine), Urine Microalbumin, Thyroid Profile, CBC and more — same-day reports.

Diabetic Foot Clinic

Neuropathy screening, vascular assessment, ulcer management, offloading, wound debridement and prevention of amputation. Specialized footwear advice.

Nutrition Counseling

Individualized diet plans by certified diabetes educators. Carbohydrate counting, weight management and cultural food adaptations for Gujarati cuisine.

Cardiac Risk Assessment

ECG, lipid management and cardiovascular risk scoring for diabetic patients to prevent heart disease.

Retinopathy Screening

Fundus examination and referral coordination for diabetic retinopathy detection and management.

Insulin & CGM Training

Hands-on training for insulin injection techniques, insulin pumps and Continuous Glucose Monitoring systems.

24×7 Emergency Care

Immediate management of hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia, DKA and other diabetes emergencies. WhatsApp & phone support available round the clock.
