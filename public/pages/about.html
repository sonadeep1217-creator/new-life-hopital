About New Life Hospital

Dedicated diabetic care centre serving Gandhidham and Kutch region since 2008

Our Story

A Hospital Built for Diabetes Care

New Life Diabetic Hospital was founded with a single mission — to provide specialized, compassionate and evidence-based diabetes care to the people of Gandhidham and surrounding areas of Kutch.

Over the years we have grown into a full-fledged diabetes care centre with dedicated OPD, advanced laboratory, diabetic foot clinic, nutrition department and 24×7 emergency support for diabetes-related crises.

We believe diabetes is manageable. With the right team, technology and patient education, every person with diabetes can lead a healthy, fulfilling life.

Hospital Highlights

  • Specialized only in Diabetes & Metabolic Disorders
  • 4 Full-time Specialist Doctors
  • NABL-standard In-house Laboratory
